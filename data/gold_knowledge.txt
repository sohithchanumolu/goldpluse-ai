Gold is considered a safe-haven asset.

Gold prices are influenced by:

- Inflation
- Interest rates
- US Dollar strength
- Central bank purchases
- Geopolitical uncertainty
- Economic recessions

Generally:

- Higher inflation often supports gold prices.
- Lower interest rates can support gold prices.
- A weaker US Dollar can increase gold prices.
- Economic uncertainty increases demand for gold.

Investors often buy gold for wealth preservation and portfolio diversification.

Gold prices can be volatile in the short term and should not be considered guaranteed investments.
