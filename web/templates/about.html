About GoldPulse AI

👁️ Vision

GoldPulse AI aims to democratize financial intelligence in the Indian Gold Market. By combining real-time data, historical tracking, and generative AI analysis, we empower retail consumers and investors to make data-driven purchasing decisions with institutional-grade insights.

🚀 Features

  • Daily 24K and 22K gold price tracking
  • USD/INR exchange rate integration
  • Historical gold price database
  • Interactive Plotly trend charts
  • AI-generated market analysis (Summary, Risk, Confidence)
  • Telegram notifications and alerts
  • FastAPI-powered dashboard
  • Ask GoldPulse AI Assistant (RAG enabled)

⚙️ Technology Stack

  • Python & FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL & SQLAlchemy
  • Plotly
  • Google Gemini AI
  • Langchain & ChromaDB
  • Telegram Bot API
  • HTML, CSS & Vanilla JS
  • Railway Deployment

📅 Timeline & Roadmap

Phase 1 (Complete)

Data Collection, Database Integration, and Basic Dashboard

Phase 2 (Complete)

Gemini AI Market Analysis, Telegram Bot Alerts

Phase 3 (Complete)

RAG AI Assistant, UI/UX Modernization, Structured Data

Phase 4 (Future)

Dark mode, price prediction models, and portfolio tracking.
